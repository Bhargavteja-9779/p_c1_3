# Data notice

The feature tables (`Target_Journal_Submission/data/features/`) and the per-cell and per-split results
(`Target_Journal_Submission/results/`) are derived from the following public data sets, each released under the
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0). The derived data are distributed under the same
licence. When using them, cite the original data and publications:

* MATR: K. A. Severson et al., Data-driven prediction of battery cycle life before capacity degradation,
  Nature Energy 4, 383-391 (2019); data https://data.matr.io/1/.
* CLO: P. M. Attia et al., Closed-loop optimization of fast-charging protocols for batteries with machine learning,
  Nature 578, 397-402 (2020); data https://data.matr.io/1/.
* HUST: G. Ma et al., Real-time personalized health status prediction of lithium-ion batteries using deep transfer
  learning, Energy & Environmental Science 15, 4083-4094 (2022); data Mendeley Data, doi:10.17632/nsc7hnsg4s.2.

The raw data are not redistributed; `Target_Journal_Submission/code/src/batscreen/data/download.py` downloads them.
